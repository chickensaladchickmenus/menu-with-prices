Chicken Salad Chick Menu with Prices

Chicken Salad Chick built its whole menu around one thing, fresh scratch-made chicken salad served the way you want it. Most individual meals sit between $9 and $11, which is fair for a fast-casual spot that uses fresh ingredients every single day. The range goes from around $6 for a kids meal all the way up to $58.49 for a catering platter, so there is something that fits most budgets.

Chicken Salad Chick prices shift a little depending on where you are, since each location runs its own rates. Everything here reflects current US averages for 2026, and the Craving Credits app is the fastest way to check the exact price at your nearest store before you head out.

How the Menu Works

If it is your first time at Chicken Salad Chick, knowing how the menu works before you get to the counter makes everything a lot easier. You pick your flavor first, then how you want it served, then a side. A pickle and a buttercream cookie come with every meal already, so you do not need to add those on your own.

More than fifteen flavors cover classic, fruity, savory, and spicy options, and you can get your chicken salad as a scoop on greens, a sandwich, a warm melt, or a Quick Chick to-go container. Once you know the format, ordering takes less than a minute.

Chicken Salad Chick Scoop Meal Prices

The Chick is the standard scoop meal and the most ordered format on the menu. It gives you one scoop of your chosen flavor served on a bed of greens alongside one Fresh Signature Side. The Chick Trio lets you try three different flavors in one visit, which makes it the smartest first order for anyone who has not been before.

Meal Format Approx Price Calories
The Chick (one scoop plus side) Around $9.49 Around 600 to 850 kcal
Chick Trio (three scoops plus side) Around $9.99 Around 900 to 1,200 kcal

The Chick Trio costs only fifty cents more than The Chick, which makes it one of the best value upgrades on the full menu since you get three times the flavor variety for almost the same price.
